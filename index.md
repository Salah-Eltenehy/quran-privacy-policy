# Privacy Policy for مصحف الوقف والإبتداء

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

دار الاحسان ("we", "us") built **مصحف الوقف والإبتداء** as a free app to help users read the Holy Quran and listen to recitations. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require registration or an account.

## 2. Device Permissions

- **Internet access:** used only to stream audio recitations and to download audio packs, fonts, and other content.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

Bookmarks, reading progress, and settings are stored locally on your device only. We cannot access them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party services that collect data.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads, no user accounts, and no third-party tracking.

## 6. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to store or disclose.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
